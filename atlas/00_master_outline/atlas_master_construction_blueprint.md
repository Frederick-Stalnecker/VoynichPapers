## MISSION

To create the definitive historical and visual atlas documenting the scientific, medical, astronomical, manuscript, and instrument traditions relevant to the Voynich Manuscript and the THEOS research program.

Atlas is not intended to replace Astromedica.

Atlas is intended to become the historical foundation upon which Astromedica rests.

---

## GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

> **Astromedica asks:** "What was discovered?"
>
> **Atlas asks:** "What world produced the conditions under which such discoveries might exist?"
>
> **Repository asks:** "What evidence exists?"

These three works form a unified research ecosystem.

## CLAIM REGISTRY

Every chapter linked to:

- **Claim IDs** — unique identifier for every factual assertion
- **Evidence IDs** — pointer to supporting evidence in the repository
- **Figure IDs** — pointer to the Figure Bible entry
- **Citation IDs** — pointer to `06_citations/`

**Rule:** No unsupported conclusion permitted.

---

## FIGURE BIBLE

Every figure assigned:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Figure Number** | Sequential, unique |
| **Source** | Origin of the image |
| **Classification** | `[HISTORICAL IMAGE]` / `[MANUSCRIPT IMAGE]` / `[RECONSTRUCTION]` / `[THEOS INTERPRETATION]` |
| **Rights Status** | Public domain / Licensed / THEOS original |
| **Caption** | Full descriptive caption |
| **Page Location** | Target page in the printed Atlas |
| **QR Target** | URL for digital companion content |
| **Claim References** | Claim IDs supported by this figure |

**Target Figure Count:** 150–250

**Repository files:** `atlas/05_figures/`
