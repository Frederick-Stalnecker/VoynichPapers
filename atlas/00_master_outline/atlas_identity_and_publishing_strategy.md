## The Core Distinction

**Astromedica** is the flagship work. It is the story. It is the discovery. It is the book that people will buy because they want to know whether the Voynich Manuscript was decoded.

**Atlas** is becoming something different.

Atlas is becoming the scholarly companion volume.

| Astromedica | Atlas |
|---|---|
| The adventure | The evidence |
| Written for readers | Written for researchers |
| "Come see what we found" | "Here is how we found it" |

Because of that distinction, Atlas should **not** be called "Astromedica Volume II."

It deserves its own identity while remaining part of the Astromedica family.

## Title Candidates

### Option 1 — The Conservative Academic Route
**ASTROMEDICA ATLAS**  
*Historical Instruments, Scientific Traditions, and the Voynich Manuscript*

The safest choice. Immediately signals its relationship to the main work.

---

### Option 2 — The Scholarly Companion Route
**THE ASTROMEDICA COMPANION**  
*A Historical Atlas of Timurid Science and the Voynich Manuscript*

Very university-friendly. Positions well for library acquisition.

---

### Option 3 — The Instrument Tradition Route
**INSTRUMENTA ASTROMEDICA**  
*The Instruments, Manuscripts, and Scientific Traditions Behind Astromedica*

Sounds like a companion volume scholars would keep next to the main work. Strong academic resonance.

---

### Option 4 — The Evidence Route
**ATLAS ASTROMEDICA**  
*An Illustrated Atlas of the Voynich Manuscript and Timurid Scientific Traditions*

Strong coffee-table atlas title. Broad appeal.

---

### Option 5 — The Full Statement
**ATLAS ASTROMEDICA**  
*An Illustrated Atlas of Timurid Science, Instruments, and the Voynich Manuscript*

**Current favorite.** Comprehensive, scholarly, and visually evocative.

## The Publishing Imprint Vision

The most significant strategic idea in this discussion is the emergence of a publishing identity:

### ASTROMEDICA PRESS

A scholarly publishing imprint that would publish:

- **Astromedica** — the flagship discovery narrative
- **Atlas Astromedica** — the illustrated scholarly companion
- **THEOS Papers** — pre-registered research findings
- **Historical reconstruction studies**
- **Instrument atlases**
- **Manuscript studies**
- **AI-assisted research monographs**

In this model, Astromedica becomes more than a book. It becomes a **publishing imprint and research program**.

## The Adversarial Caution

The right-engine critique raises one essential point:

> Do not tie Atlas exclusively to a single interpretation.

If Atlas remains a rigorous historical atlas that clearly separates:

- `[HISTORICAL]` evidence
- `[INSTRUMENT]` tradition
- `[COMPARISON]` analysis
- `[THEOS]` findings

then the book retains value even for readers who disagree with portions of the conclusions.

**This dramatically increases its lifespan.**

Fifty years from now, a historian might still cite Atlas for its instrument research even if parts of the Voynich debate evolve. That is the mark of a durable scholarly work.
